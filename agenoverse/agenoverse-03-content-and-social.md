## Rhythm (starting point)

LinkedIn 3 a week · Instagram 3 to 4 plus stories · Facebook 3 · X 4 to 5. Consistency beats volume.

## Caption structure

1. **Hook:** one short line that makes people stop.
2. **Value:** 2 to 4 short lines on what it is and why it matters.
3. **Call to action:** one clear next step.
4. **Hashtags:** #Agenoverse plus #LetsRiseTogether or #LetsDoIt where they fit, and 2 or 3 topic tags (#AI, #Pakistan, #TechCareers). About 5 in total.

On LinkedIn and Facebook, paste the link in the post. On Instagram, say "link in bio".

## Formats that work

- **Carousels** get more engagement than single images; 9 to 12 slides tend to do best (Socialinsider, Aug 2026). Use them for tips and roadmaps.
- On LinkedIn, upload carousels as a **PDF document post**.
- X allows only **4 images per post**; use the strongest slides or a thread.

## Tools

- **ChatGPT (free):** writing copy, captions, slide text. Free image generation is limited to a few images a day.
- **Gemini:** optional text-free background art.
- **Canva (free):** templates and final assembly. Always add text and the logo in Canva, never inside AI images (AI renders text and logos badly).
- **Scheduling:** Meta Business Suite (Facebook, Instagram); Buffer free or native schedulers (LinkedIn, X).
