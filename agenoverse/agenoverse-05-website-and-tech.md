## How we build (AI-native SDLC)

Every build follows Anthropic's AI-native SDLC with Claude Code:
1. `intent.md`: what and why
2. `spec.md`: exact requirements
3. `plan.md`: phased build steps
4. Build in phases, test, review

This same method is what Agenoversity teaches: use AI inside a proper process instead of asking it to build everything at once.
