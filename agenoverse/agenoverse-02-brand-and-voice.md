# Agenoverse: Brand and Voice

> Use these rules for anything public: posts, captions, slides, website copy, emails, designs and image prompts. These are decided; don't change them.

## Names (write exactly like this)

| Name | What it is | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agenoverse | The organization | Never AgenoVerse, Ageno Verse, or AGENOVERSE in body text |
| Agenoverse Community | The skills-first community | Or "the community" |
| Agenoversity | Learning platform | One word |
| Ageno Labs | Project and services arm | Two words, plural |
| Let's Rise Together | The campaign | Keep the apostrophe |
| Let's Do It | The challenges | "Let's Do It challenges" |

## Voice

Professional and friendly, community-first: a well-run team that welcomes you in. Not a corporate pitch, not hype. English only.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Speak as "we" and invite people in | Talk down to beginners or sound exclusive |
| Be specific: what, when, how to join | Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, 10x |
| Be honest that we're new and starting now | Claim jobs, partners, member counts or results we don't have |
| Encourage people who are struggling | Mock people for using or not using AI |
| Short, clear sentences | Unexplained jargon |
| At most 1 or 2 emoji per post | Walls of emoji and hashtags |

Sample lines:
- "Struggling to break into tech in the AI era? You're not alone. Let's rise together."
- "Using AI isn't the hard part. Knowing what to build, and why it works, is."
- "We're starting small, and you're early. Join the caravan."

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | #0E1116 | Dark base, default post background |
| Bone | #F6F5F1 | Light base: tips and Ageno Labs posts |
| Teal | #22C3B0 | Brand accent on ink; the logo cursor |
| Teal deep | #0F8B7F | Accent on bone, large text only |
| Amber | #F5B544 | Community and campaign moments only, max about 5% of a design |

**Rule:** teal is the system, amber is the people. No other colours, no gradients.
Never: teal text on bone, white text on amber or teal, amber text on bone. On amber, use ink text.

## Type

- **Geist** for headlines (semibold, slightly tight spacing) and body (regular).
- **Geist Mono** for tags and labels (medium, uppercase) and code.
- On a 1080 px post: headline 88 px, subhead 52, body 36, small 28, tag 28.
- One idea per post; headlines 8 words or fewer.

## Logo

- **Cursor A:** a terminal-style letter A whose crossbar is a teal block cursor.
- Versions: mark alone, mark + "Agenoverse" wordmark, and the **emblem** (the A inside an orbit with three dots: amber = Community, teal = Ageno Labs, bone/ink = Agenoversity).
- On dark: bone A, teal cursor. On light: ink A, teal-deep cursor.
- Profile pictures use the plain A on ink. The emblem leads covers and launch posts. The wordmark logo sits top left on posts.
- Never stretch, recolour, outline, add shadows or glows, redraw, or place on a busy photo.
- All official files live in the Agenoverse design system (Logos, Profile pictures, Covers, First posts).

## Post layout

- Feed posts **1080 × 1350 (4:5)**; stories 1080 × 1920 (keep top and bottom 310 px clear).
- Same zones every time: logo top left, tag, headline, supporting line, call to action at bottom, 64 px margin.
- Templates: Announcement (ink), Let's Do It challenge (ink + amber tag), Tip / carousel (bone + teal deep), Ageno Labs showcase (bone + real screenshot), Event / spotlight (real photo + ink band).

## Imagery

Use real photos of people, real screenshots of builds, and simple flat graphics in brand colours.
Avoid AI stock art: glowing brains, robots, neon circuits, anything that looks AI-generated. Label AI-made images if they could be mistaken for real photos.

## Red lines

- No made-up results, members, partners, placements or testimonials.
- Ask permission before featuring anyone; blur children's faces at school sessions unless the school and parents agree in writing.
- Stay out of politics and religion debates.
- Never share private data of members or clients.
